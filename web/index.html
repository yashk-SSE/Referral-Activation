Inside Online and Others

The two Sub-Channels that are not one population. Online splits on whether a utm_campaign prompted the referral; Others splits on the underlying referral source.

Campaign-driven referrals carry a utm_campaign (WhatsApp blasts, brand search, promos); unprompted ones carry none. What remains in Others is mostly routes with no employee role by design — only the unattributed row is genuinely unknown.
