Month on month —

Every metric for the selection, split by installation month. Customers sit in the month their system was installed, and their referrals are then counted inside that customer’s own activation window — so a column is a cohort, not a calendar of referrals.

Recent months read low because most of their 90-day window has not elapsed yet. Click any count to download that month’s customers; percentages and per-referrer ratios are derived, so they are not clickable.

Solar Consultants —

Each consultant’s own installed base and how much of it activated. A customer is attributed to the consultant on their order (lead.assigned_sc), so this is the book they handed over, not who chased the referral afterwards.

The picker lists only the consultants working the selected cluster. Tick one or several — or click a name below — to narrow the month-on-month table to those books. Consultants with a very small base swing wildly on percentage: read Act % alongside the base, never alone.
